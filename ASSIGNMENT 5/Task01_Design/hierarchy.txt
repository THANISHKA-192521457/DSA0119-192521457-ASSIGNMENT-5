Bill (abstract)
  data: patientId, patientName
  pure virtual: total() const, type() const
  virtual: print() const, destructor
  |
  +-- OutPatientBill: consultation + tests
  +-- InPatientBill: days * roomRate + procedure + medicines
  +-- DayCareBill: packagePrice
  +-- EmergencyBill: baseCharge + hourlyCharge * hours

All derived total(), type() functions override the base interface.
