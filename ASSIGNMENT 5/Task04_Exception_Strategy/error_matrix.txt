Error condition                  Recommended handling
Negative charge                  InvalidCharge
Claim exceeds policy cover       ClaimError
Unknown government package       UnknownPackage
Missing patient record            MissingPatientRecord
Text instead of numeric input     std::invalid_argument from std::stod
Allocation failure                std::bad_alloc
Validation/parsing/allocation functions may throw. Simple accessors and destructors should
not throw. Destructors should be noexcept so stack unwinding cannot be interrupted.
